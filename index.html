Bridging 1879 to Early Schools

Quick Recall Challenge

What was Wundt's primary method for analyzing conscious experience, and why did later psychologists criticize it?

Introspection: Self-reporting conscious thoughts. Criticized for being too subjective, unreliable, and unobservable.

Shift in Perspective

How did disagreeing over introspection lead to entirely new psychological schools (Behaviorism, Psychoanalysis, Gestalt)?

Phase II: The First Schools of Psychology Time: 20 Minutes

Explore the 5 foundational schools of thought that shaped modern psychology:

1. Structuralism

Key Figure: Edward Titchener (and Wilhelm Wundt)

Focus: Breaking conscious experience down into its basic structure/elements using introspection.

2. Functionalism

Key Figure: William James

Focus: How mental processes function to help organisms adapt to their environment (influenced by Darwin).

3. Psychoanalysis

Key Figure: Sigmund Freud

Focus: Unconscious drives, early childhood experiences, and repressed conflicts shaping behavior.

4. Behaviorism

Key Figures: John B. Watson, B.F. Skinner

Focus: Rejection of consciousness; study of strictly observable behavior and environmental learning.

5. Gestalt Psychology

Key Figures: Max Wertheimer, Wolfgang Köhler

Focus: Perception and mind as a unified whole. "The whole is greater than the sum of its parts."

🧠 Quick Check: Which School?

"Psychology should strictly study observable actions and ignore internal mental states." Which school fits this statement?

Phase III: Cooperative Comparative Matrix Time: 20 Minutes

Group Activity (Teams of 3-4): Roles: Researcher, Matrix Scribe, Presenter. Compare the five early schools across key dimensions.

School of Thought Key Author(s) Central Idea / Focus Subject of Study
Structuralism Edward Titchener, W. Wundt Analyze mind into basic elements. Conscious experience (Introspection)
Functionalism William James Purpose/function of mental processes. Adaptive mental and behavioral processes
Psychoanalysis Sigmund Freud Unconscious mind drives behavior. Unconscious motives & early childhood
Behaviorism John B. Watson, B.F. Skinner Only measurable behavior counts. Observable behavior & environment
Gestalt Max Wertheimer Organized wholes in perception. Perception and unified consciousness

Team Challenge Prompt

Case Analysis: Imagine a student is struggling with fear of public speaking. How would a Behaviorist vs. a Psychoanalyst explain the root cause of this fear differently?

Behaviorist: Fear was learned through past conditioning/negative experiences.
Psychoanalyst: Fear stems from an unconscious conflict or unresolved childhood event.
